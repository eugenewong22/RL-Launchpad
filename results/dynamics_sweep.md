# Robustness to dynamics the policy never trained on

The reported FetchPush policies (3 seeds, trained on stock dynamics only) evaluated under shifted block mass and sliding friction. Exact R4 protocol — 50 episodes, eval seeds 10000+, deterministic policy — with only the simulator's physical parameters changed. Generated by `scripts/dynamics_sweep.py`.

×1.0 is the reported condition and is included as the sweep's own control: it reproduces the headline 0.993.
